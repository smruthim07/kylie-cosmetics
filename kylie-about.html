Our Story

STARTED WITH
A Lip Kit.

In 2015, Kylie Jenner launched a single product with one idea — beauty should feel personal. What happened next changed everything.

"I've always been passionate about makeup. This brand is everything I wished I had growing up — and everything I want to give to you."

— Kylie Jenner, Founder & CEO

Kylie Cosmetics launched in November 2015 with a single product: a matte lip kit. The first drop sold out in minutes — within weeks it became a cultural moment, not just a beauty launch.

Today, Kylie Cosmetics spans cosmetics, skincare, and fragrance. Every product is built on one belief: beauty should feel personal, playful, and entirely yours.

The Journey

2015

The lip kit that started it all

Kylie Jenner launches her first Matte Lip Kit. 15,000 units sell out in under a minute. The beauty internet has never been the same.

2016

Expanding the universe

The brand grows beyond lip colour — concealers, eyeshadow palettes, and the iconic Kylie Holiday collection launch to massive demand.

2019

Kylie Skin is born

A full skincare line — vegan, cruelty-free, and built on the principle that great makeup starts with great skin.

2023

Cosmic — the first fragrance

Kylie ventures into fine fragrance. An Eau de Parfum as bold, warm, and unforgettable as the woman behind it.

Now

A full beauty universe

From lips to skin to scent — Kylie Cosmetics continues to grow, innovate, and put beauty back in the hands of the people who wear it.
